Tab & column mapping

Defaults below match your Registrations and DivClass tabs. Class and Order are formulas in your sheet (an IFS lookup and a VLOOKUP) — this app reads them but never overwrites them, so your existing automation stays intact. Adjust letters below only if your columns move.

Columns D and E on your DivClass tab (DivClass and DivClassOrder) are formulas built from the fields above — this app reads them only for reference and never writes to them. Height is read from your Height Inches column (L by default) as a plain number of inches, and is only written back on save for a row if you actually edited it in the app — if you leave a row's height untouched, whatever formula is already in that cell (like =(H2*12)+I2) is left completely alone. Checked-in is written as TRUE/FALSE to your existing Checked-In column. Total and Rank in Class are written by the Scoring page's "Save scores to sheet" button — each competitor's total and rank are computed within their own class only. Your sheet also has "social media" (E) and "add division" (G) columns that aren't wired up to anything here — let me know if you'd like those brought into the app.

One-click cleanup

Trims stray whitespace, fixes name/division capitalization, fuzzy-matches division text against your DivClass tab, and flags rows with missing or non-numeric height/weight — the usual copy-paste-from-another-system mess. Only writes to Name, Division, Height, Weight, Shirt Size, Checked-in, Athlete Num — never to Class, Order, or Total, since those are your formulas.

Scoring

Pick a division, then enter judges' scores class by class as each is called. Every class within the division is scored, totaled, and ranked separately from the others — a class's ranking never mixes with another class in the same division. Writes to your Judge, Total, and Rank in Class columns. Competitors who haven't checked in are excluded here automatically.

Each judge places competitors 1 through the number of competitors in that competitor's own class (no ties, no skipped numbers) — or enters 999 for a competitor who isn't being ranked (withdrew, no-showed, etc.), which drops them from ranking but keeps them in the sheet. Always ordered by Athlete Num within each class. Press Enter to move down a judge's column; at the bottom of a class it jumps to the top of that class's next judge column. Saving writes each competitor's Total and their Rank in Class — both computed within their own class only. "Number of judges today" hides the unused judge columns below (e.g. set it to 5 if two judges are out) without losing any scores already entered in them — raise it again and those columns and their scores reappear. "Recalculate totals" refreshes the on-screen Total column and ranking for scores that were already sitting on a class or division when you loaded it — handy when you step away and come back to one instead of retyping something to trigger a refresh.
