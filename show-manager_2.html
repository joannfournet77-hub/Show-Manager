Tab & column mapping

Defaults below match your Registrations and DivClass tabs. Class and Order are formulas in your sheet (an IFS lookup and a VLOOKUP) — this app reads them but never overwrites them, so your existing automation stays intact. Adjust letters below only if your columns move.

Columns C, F, and G on your DivClass tab (Full Division, DivClass, and DivClassOrder) are formulas built from the fields above — this app reads them only for reference and never writes to them. The Registrations page shows a single combined Height (in) field for editing convenience; behind the scenes it's split back into Feet + Inches on save, since your M column formula depends on those two cells and is never overwritten directly. Checked-in is written as TRUE/FALSE — add that header to your sheet once, in any open column, and point the mapping above at it.

Setup

Your Order code (e.g. 2-B) is built by your sheet's formula from two lookup tables — edit those tables here and the order follows automatically, same as it does in your sheet.

▾Division order

Drag to reorder. This is the number before the dash in the order code — which division goes on stage first, second, etc.

▾Class order

Drag to reorder. This is shared across every division — it's the letter after the dash (Teen, Masters, and weight/height classes all get a position here once, used everywhere they appear).

▾Divisions & classes

Threshold is weight (lbs) for Bodybuilding classes, height (inches) for Classic Physique / Fit Physique / Physique / Figure / Wellness / Bikini classes, and left blank for any Teen or Masters class in any division, and for Transformation classes — none of those split by height or weight. The computed order code shown per row comes from the two tables above; it isn't editable directly.

Registrations

Clean up entries and manage competitors. Class & Order stay as your sheet's own formulas compute them — this just checks them.

One-click cleanup

Trims stray whitespace, fixes name/division capitalization, fuzzy-matches division text against your DivClass tab, and flags rows with missing or non-numeric height/weight — the usual copy-paste-from-another-system mess. Only writes to Name, Division, Height, Weight, Shirt Size, Checked-in, Athlete Num — never to Class, Order, or Total, since those are your formulas.

Competitors

Click a column header to sort. Drag the edge of a header to resize it. Athlete Num can be edited directly to correct it after auto-numbering. Uncheck "Checked-in" to exclude a competitor from Scoring and from printed reports (they stay in the sheet). Use "+ Class" on a row to enter the same competitor into another class, copying their height/weight. "Class (live)" and "Order (live)" recalculate instantly as you edit height, weight, or division, following the same rules as Setup — compare them against "Class (sheet)" / "Order (sheet)" (highlighted when they differ) to see what the row will become once you save and the sheet's own formula recalculates.

Scoring

Enter judges' scores for a class as it's called. Writes to your Judge columns only — Total is left to your sheet. Competitors who haven't checked in are excluded here automatically.

Each judge places competitors 1 through the number of competitors in the class (no ties, no skipped numbers) — or enters 999 for a competitor who isn't being ranked (withdrew, no-showed, etc.), which drops them from ranking but keeps them in the sheet. Always ordered by Athlete Num. Press Enter to move down a judge's column; at the bottom it jumps to the top of the next judge's column.
